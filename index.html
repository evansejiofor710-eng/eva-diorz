THE ARTIST / 02

More than
a sound.

Eva Diorz is an emerging Nigerian artist exploring the meeting point of Afrobeat, Afro-fusion, Amapiano and rap. Driven by rhythm, honest expression and a love for music, Eva is building a distinct sound and a story that keeps moving forward.

Rooted in ambition and inspired by real-life experiences, the music is made to connect, uplift and leave its mark.
